# Lab 6 decision note

## Part A — Initial batch

- What question is the depot manager asking?
- How many pages, delivered rows, unique readings, and duplicate deliveries did you find?
- What are the source coverage, usable and fresh coverage, and invalid-reading rate? Show each numerator and denominator.
- Which rules passed or failed? Is your decision ACCEPT or HOLD? Explain using your results.
- What should the manager do next? What can this batch not tell them?

## Part B — Retry and processing choice

- Compare the initial and retry results. Did the release decision change? Why?
- What happened when you reran the retry batch? Describe how you checked for duplicate output.
- For a report reviewed every 15 minutes, would a scheduled batch fit? Explain.
- For an alert required within one minute, would that schedule fit? Explain a possible streaming approach and one limitation.
